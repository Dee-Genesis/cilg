About Country Directors

CILG's Global Representatives

Country Directors are distinguished professionals appointed by CILG to lead the Institute's operations, membership development, and professional standards activities within their designated countries. They serve as the primary point of contact for prospective members, Accredited Learning Partners, and institutional stakeholders in their regions.

Each Country Director is selected on the basis of their professional standing, demonstrated expertise in leadership and governance, and their commitment to advancing CILG's mission within their national context.

Country Directors work closely with the Board of Governors and CILG's Executive leadership to implement the Institute's strategic objectives at the national level — facilitating local membership growth, supporting professional development activities, and promoting the value of CILG's credentials within their communities.

Their appointment reflects CILG's commitment to building a truly global professional body rooted in local knowledge, regional expertise, and meaningful community engagement.

Mandate

Role of Country Directors

Country Directors carry out a vital mandate — connecting CILG's global standards to local professional communities and driving meaningful impact on the ground.

🤝
Membership Growth
Identify, recruit, and support prospective CILG members within their country, championing the value of CILG credentials and professional designations among local professionals and institutions.
🏛️
Institutional Representation
Act as CILG's official representative in interactions with government bodies, universities, professional associations, and corporate organisations at the national level.
📚
Professional Development
Facilitate and support local professional development activities, CPD events, and learning partnerships — ensuring members have access to high-quality governance education and training.
