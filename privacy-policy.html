Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Policy explains how the Chartered Institute of Leadership and Governance collects, uses, protects, and shares your personal data, and sets out your rights under applicable data protection law.

Section 1

Introduction

On This Page — Contents
1 Introduction2 Information We Collect3 How We Use Data4 Legal Basis5 Sharing & Disclosure6 Retention7 Your Rights8 Cookies9 Security10 International11 Children12 Contact

The Chartered Institute of Leadership and Governance ("CILG", "we", "our") is committed to protecting personal data of members, applicants, website visitors, and other users. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, retention, and your rights. It applies to our website at www.cilgglobal.org, member portals, and all services. Read alongside our Terms of Use and Membership Framework.

Policy Updates: Material changes will be notified to members via email. Continued use after notice constitutes acceptance.

CILG acts as data controller. Third-party processors operate under data processing agreements.

Section 2

Information We Collect

Categories include:

2.1 Directly Provided

  • Full name, title, address, email, phone
  • Date of birth, nationality
  • Professional background, qualifications, references
  • Payment information (processed securely)

2.2 Automatically

  • IP address, browser type, pages visited, device data
  • Cookie data (see Section 8)

2.3 Third Parties

  • Professional references, verification from regulatory bodies, public professional profiles, partner institutions

Special Category Data: Not collected unless strictly necessary and with explicit consent or lawful basis.

Section 5

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your data. Sharing occurs with: service providers (payment, email, hosting, assessment platforms), public register (name, grade, status via verification portal), legal/regulatory authorities, and professional conduct committees. No commercial transfer.

No Sale: CILG does not sell personal data to third parties.

Section 8

Cookies & Tracking

We use necessary, functional, analytics (Google Analytics), and optional marketing cookies. Manage via browser settings or consent tool. Disabling may affect functionality.

Section 9

Data Security

TLS encryption, PCI-DSS payment processing, ISO 27001 hosting, access controls, regular training. Breach notification provided as required by law.

Section 10

International Transfers

Data transferred to USA and other jurisdictions where CILG or its service providers operate. Safeguards include adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses, or your consent.

Section 11

Children's Privacy

Services are for adults. Under 18 requires parental consent for Student programmes. Contact us if data collected without consent.
