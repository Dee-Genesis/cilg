Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Policy explains how the Chartered Institute of Leadership and Governance collects, uses, protects, and shares your personal data, and sets out your rights under applicable data protection law.

Section 1

Introduction

On This Page — Table of Contents

The Chartered Institute of Leadership and Governance ("CILG", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the personal data of all individuals who interact with the Institute — whether as members, applicants, students, website visitors, event participants, or other users of our services.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, the purposes for which we use it, how long we keep it, and with whom we may share it. It also describes the rights you hold over your personal data and how to exercise those rights.

This Policy applies to all personal data processed by CILG through our website at www.cilgglobal.org, our membership and application portals, digital communications, and all services delivered to members and the public. It should be read alongside our Terms of Use and the Membership Framework.

Policy Updates: We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Where changes are material, we will notify existing members via their registered email address. The date of the most recent revision is shown at the top of this page. Continued use of CILG services after notification of changes constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

CILG acts as the data controller in respect of the personal data it collects and processes. Where we engage third-party service providers to process data on our behalf, we do so under appropriate data processing agreements that require those parties to protect your information in accordance with applicable law.

Section 2

Information We Collect

We collect personal information through a variety of channels depending on your relationship with CILG. The categories of personal data we may collect and process include the following.

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

This includes data you submit when creating an account, applying for membership, registering for a qualification or event, contacting us, or completing any online form:

  • Full name, title, and preferred name;
  • Postal address, email address, and telephone number;
  • Date of birth and nationality;
  • Professional background, employment history, and educational qualifications;
  • Copies of identification documents, academic certificates, and professional references;
  • Payment and billing information (processed securely via our payment providers);
  • Communications sent to us, including enquiries, complaints, and feedback.

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you access our websites or digital portals, we may automatically collect certain technical data:

  • IP address, browser type, and operating system;
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and referral sources;
  • Device identifiers and session data;
  • Cookie and tracking data (see Section 8 for details).

2.3 Information from Third Parties

In some cases we may receive personal data about you from third parties, including:

  • Professional references submitted in support of a membership or qualification application;
  • Verification data from regulatory bodies, professional registries, or licensing authorities;
  • Publicly available professional profiles (e.g. LinkedIn) where relevant to the assessment of an application;
  • Partner organisations and accreditation bodies with whom CILG maintains formal arrangements.

Special Category Data: We do not routinely collect sensitive personal data (such as health information, racial or ethnic origin, or political opinions) unless this is strictly necessary for a specific, identified purpose and we have obtained your explicit consent or have another lawful basis for doing so.

Section 3

How We Use Your Information

We use the personal data we collect only for legitimate purposes directly connected to our role as a professional body. The principal purposes for which we process your data are as follows.

Purpose Description
Membership Administration Processing applications, issuing membership credentials, managing renewals, and maintaining the CILG Member Register.
Qualification & Certification Enrolling candidates, administering assessments, issuing certificates, and managing CPD records.
Communications Sending administrative notices, renewal reminders, policy updates, and other information material to your relationship with CILG.
Public Register & Verification Publishing your name, credential, and membership status on the CILG public register and Verification Portal.
Events & Training Organising and delivering conferences, workshops, webinars, and professional development programmes.
Financial Transactions Processing membership fees, examination fees, and event registration payments, and maintaining financial records.
Legal Compliance Meeting our obligations under applicable law, responding to legal proceedings, and co-operating with regulatory authorities.
Disciplinary Proceedings Investigating complaints, conducting professional conduct reviews, and maintaining records of disciplinary outcomes.
Website & Portal Improvement Analysing usage patterns to improve the functionality, security, and user experience of our digital platforms.
Marketing & Newsletters Sending news, publications, and promotional content where you have opted in or where we have a legitimate interest in keeping you informed of relevant developments.

We will not use your personal data for purposes that are incompatible with those listed above without first seeking your consent or establishing another lawful basis for doing so.

Section 4

Legal Basis for Processing

Where data protection law requires us to identify a legal basis for processing your personal data, we rely on one or more of the following grounds depending on the nature of the processing activity.

Processing Grounds

Contract Processing is necessary to perform a contract to which you are party, or to take steps at your request before entering into a contract — for example, administering your membership or delivering a qualification programme.
Legal Obligation Processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which CILG is subject — for example, maintaining financial records or co-operating with regulatory authorities.
Legitimate Interests Processing is necessary for our legitimate interests as a professional body, such as maintaining the integrity of our public register, improving our digital services, or communicating relevant professional news to members, provided these interests are not overridden by your rights.
Consent Where we rely on your consent — for example, for optional marketing communications or special category data — we will ask for your explicit agreement. You may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
Public Task Certain processing activities are undertaken in the exercise of our functions as a recognised professional standards body, including the maintenance of public registers of professional credentials.
Vital Interests In exceptional circumstances, we may process personal data to protect the vital interests of an individual where other legal bases are not applicable — for example, in a medical emergency at a CILG event.
Section 5

Sharing & Disclosure of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We may share your information only in the limited circumstances described below.

5.1 Service Providers

We engage trusted third-party organisations to provide services on our behalf. These include:

  • Payment processors for the secure handling of membership and examination fees;
  • Email and communications platforms for the delivery of administrative and marketing messages;
  • Hosting and cloud infrastructure providers for the operation of our websites and portals;
  • Assessment and e-learning platforms used in the delivery of CILG qualifications;
  • Event management systems for the organisation of conferences and professional development activities.

All service providers are required to process your data only in accordance with our instructions and to maintain appropriate technical and organisational security measures.

5.2 Public Register

As a professional body, CILG maintains a public register of its members. By accepting membership, you consent to your full name, membership grade, credential designations, and membership status (active, suspended, or lapsed) being accessible to the public via the CILG Verification Portal. If you have specific concerns about publication of your details, please contact us at privacy@cilgglobal.org.

5.3 Legal and Regulatory Disclosure

We may disclose your personal data where required to do so by applicable law, court order, or regulatory authority, or where we reasonably believe such disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of CILG, its members, or the public.

5.4 Professional Conduct Proceedings

In the context of a disciplinary or professional conduct investigation, personal data relating to the subject of the complaint and any witnesses or complainants may be shared with members of the CILG Professional Standards Committee and relevant legal advisers on a strictly confidential basis.

No Sale of Data: CILG does not engage in the sale or commercial transfer of personal data to third parties for marketing or commercial purposes. Any sharing of data with partner organisations is subject to strict contractual safeguards and is limited to what is necessary for the specific purpose.

5.5 Business Transfers

In the unlikely event that CILG undergoes a restructuring, merger, or transfer of its functions to a successor body, your personal data may be transferred as part of that process. We will notify affected individuals in advance and ensure that appropriate data protection obligations are maintained by any successor organisation.

Section 6

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law or professional standards obligations. The following general retention periods apply.

Data Category Retention Period Rationale
Active membership records Duration of membership + 7 years Legal compliance, audit purposes, and professional record integrity.
Qualification & certification records Indefinite (permanent archive) Professional credentials must remain verifiable throughout a member's career.
Unsuccessful applications 2 years from date of decision To enable appeals and re-application processes.
Financial & payment records 7 years from transaction date Statutory accounting and tax compliance obligations.
Disciplinary records 10 years from resolution Professional standards accountability and regulatory obligations.
Event registration data 3 years from the event For CPD logging and event history purposes.
Website analytics data 26 months on a rolling basis Industry-standard period for digital analytics and performance review.
Marketing opt-in records Until consent is withdrawn + 1 year Compliance with consent management requirements.

After the applicable retention period, personal data is securely deleted or anonymised. Where anonymisation is used in preference to deletion, the resulting data no longer constitutes personal data and may be retained and used for research or statistical purposes.

Section 7

Your Rights

Depending on your location and the applicable data protection framework, you may hold one or more of the following rights in respect of your personal data held by CILG. We are committed to honouring these rights promptly and without undue bureaucracy.

📋

Right of Access

You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, together with information about how it is processed. This is commonly known as a Subject Access Request (SAR).

✏️

Right to Rectification

You may request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you. We encourage members to keep their profile details current through the Member Portal.

🗑️

Right to Erasure

In certain circumstances, you may request that we delete your personal data. This right is not absolute and may be outweighed by our legal obligations or legitimate interests as a professional body.

⏸️

Right to Restriction

You may ask us to restrict the processing of your data — for example, while the accuracy of the data is being contested or an objection is pending.

📦

Right to Portability

Where processing is based on your consent or a contract, you may request that we provide your data in a structured, machine-readable format for transfer to another organisation.

🚫

Right to Object

You may object to processing carried out on the basis of our legitimate interests, or to the use of your data for direct marketing purposes. We will cease marketing immediately on receipt of an objection.

🤖

Automated Decision-Making

You have the right not to be subject to decisions made solely by automated means where those decisions have a significant legal or similar effect on you. CILG does not currently use fully automated decision-making in its membership processes.

↩️

Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

How to Exercise Your Rights: To exercise any of the rights listed above, please submit your request in writing to privacy@cilgglobal.org. We will acknowledge your request within 5 business days and respond in full within 30 calendar days (or within any shorter period required by applicable law). We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

Section 8

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website and digital portals use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyse traffic patterns, and support the secure operation of our platforms. This section explains what cookies are, which types we use, and how you can manage your preferences.

8.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a web server when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your preferences and actions over time, and to distinguish your browser from others visiting the site.

8.2 Categories of Cookies We Use

Cookie Types

Strictly Necessary Essential for the operation of our website and portals — for example, session authentication, security tokens, and load balancing. These cannot be disabled without affecting functionality.
Functional Used to remember your preferences, such as language settings and accessibility choices, to provide a more personalised experience across repeat visits.
Analytics & Performance Allow us to measure and improve the performance of our website by collecting anonymised data on pages visited, time on site, and error occurrences. We use Google Analytics and comparable tools for this purpose.
Marketing & Targeting Used only where you have provided explicit consent, these cookies allow us to deliver relevant content and communications. We do not use third-party advertising networks to track your activity outside our own platforms.

8.3 Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through our on-site consent management tool, which is displayed when you first visit our website. You may also control cookies through your browser settings; however, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our platforms. Instructions for managing cookies in common browsers are available via your browser's help documentation.

Please note that strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled as they are integral to the secure operation of our services.

Section 9

Data Security

The security of your personal data is a priority for CILG. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, destruction, alteration, or disclosure.

9.1 Technical Safeguards

  • All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security);
  • Member portal access is protected by secure authentication controls, including password hashing and session management;
  • Payment data is processed exclusively through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways — CILG does not store card numbers or financial credentials on its own systems;
  • Our hosting infrastructure is maintained within ISO 27001-certified data centres with physical and environmental security controls.

9.2 Organisational Measures

  • Access to personal data is restricted to CILG staff and authorised contractors who have a legitimate need to access it in the course of their duties;
  • All personnel with access to personal data receive data protection training commensurate with their role;
  • We conduct periodic reviews of our data processing activities and security practices.

Data Breach Notification: In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify affected individuals without undue delay and in accordance with the requirements of applicable data protection law. You may also report a suspected breach or security concern to us at privacy@cilgglobal.org.

While we take all reasonable precautions, no internet transmission or electronic storage system is completely secure. We encourage you to use strong, unique passwords for your CILG portal account and to contact us immediately if you suspect any unauthorised access to your account.

Section 10

International Data Transfers

CILG is a global professional body with members and operations across multiple jurisdictions. As a result, your personal data may be transferred to, and stored or processed in, countries other than your country of residence.

Where such transfers occur, we take steps to ensure that your data receives an equivalent level of protection as it does in your home jurisdiction. The mechanisms we rely on for international transfers include:

  • Adequacy decisions issued by relevant data protection authorities recognising the destination country's level of protection;
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by applicable supervisory authorities, incorporated into our agreements with international service providers;
  • Binding Corporate Rules or equivalent intra-group data transfer mechanisms where applicable;
  • Your explicit consent, in circumstances where no other appropriate safeguard applies.

CILG's primary operational and data hosting infrastructure is based in the United States of America. Members located outside the USA should be aware that their data will be transferred to and processed within the USA in the ordinary course of membership administration.

Queries on International Transfers: If you would like further information about the specific safeguards applied to the transfer of your personal data, or a copy of the relevant transfer mechanism, please contact us at privacy@cilgglobal.org.

Section 11

Children's Privacy

CILG's services are designed for and directed at professional adults. We do not knowingly collect or process personal data from individuals under the age of 18 without the verified consent of a parent or legal guardian.

Individuals under 18 may only participate in CILG Student programmes under the following conditions:

  • Prior written consent from a parent or legal guardian must be obtained and retained by CILG before enrolment;
  • Data collected from minors is limited to what is strictly necessary for the educational programme in question;
  • A parent or guardian may at any time request access to, rectification of, or deletion of their child's personal data by contacting us at privacy@cilgglobal.org.

If you believe that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child under the age of 18 without appropriate consent, please contact us immediately so that we can take appropriate steps, including deletion of the data where required.

12.1 Supervisory Authority Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response to your enquiry or complaint, or if you believe that CILG is processing your personal data in breach of applicable law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection supervisory authority in your country of residence or place of work.

United States Residents

While the USA does not yet have a single federal data protection authority, certain state privacy laws — including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) — may give you additional rights. Residents of states with applicable privacy legislation may contact CILG to exercise rights under those laws. We will respond to all such requests in accordance with the relevant statutory requirements.

UK & European Economic Area Residents

If you are located in the UK or EEA and believe your data has been processed in contravention of the UK GDPR or EU GDPR, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK at ico.org.uk, or with the supervisory authority in your EU member state of habitual residence.

CILG reserves the right to amend this Privacy Policy at any time. All updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Where changes are material, we will provide prior notice to existing members via their registered email address. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically to stay informed of any updates.
