CILG post-nominal designations and professional credentials are awarded to individuals who have demonstrated the requisite knowledge, experience, and professional standing as assessed by the Institute. All credentials remain the property of CILG and are granted to Members on a conditional, non-transferable, and revocable licence basis.

5.2 Conditions of Use

Members holding a CILG credential may use the associated post-nominal designation only whilst their membership is active and in good standing. The following conditions apply to the use of all CILG credentials:

  • Post-nominal designations must be used accurately and in the form authorised by CILG. Abbreviations or modified forms not approved by the Institute are prohibited;

CILG maintains a public Verification Portal through which the credentials and membership status of any CILG member can be verified by third parties. Members consent to their name, credential, membership grade, and good-standing status being included in the CILG Register and being accessible via the Verification Portal.

Misuse of Credentials: Any fraudulent claim to hold a CILG credential or the use of a revoked credential is a serious breach of these Terms and may be referred to the relevant professional, regulatory, or law enforcement authorities. CILG reserves the right to pursue all appropriate legal remedies in such cases.

5.4 Revocation of Credentials

CILG may revoke any credential at any time on the grounds of: misrepresentation in the original application; breach of the Code of Professional Conduct; failure to maintain CPD requirements; non-payment of dues; criminal conviction relevant to professional standing; or any other conduct deemed by CILG to be incompatible with membership. Revocation is effective from the date of written notification. Members retain no right to use a revoked credential after that date.

Termination & Suspension

11.1 Voluntary Resignation

A Member may resign from CILG membership at any time by submitting written notice to the Membership Office at membership@cilgglobal.org. Resignation takes effect from the date of acknowledgement by CILG. No refund of subscription fees already paid will be due upon voluntary resignation. Any outstanding dues remain payable notwithstanding resignation.

11.2 Suspension

CILG may suspend a Member's membership and all associated rights and privileges, with immediate effect, where:

  • A formal complaint or investigation is pending before the Professional Standards Committee;
  • The Member has failed to meet CPD requirements;
  • Subscription fees are overdue by more than 30 days;
  • There is reason to believe the Member has breached these Terms or the Code of Professional Conduct;
  • A criminal investigation or regulatory proceeding relevant to professional standing is pending or ongoing.

During suspension, the Member may not represent themselves as a CILG member in good standing or use any CILG post-nominal designation.

11.3 Termination by CILG

CILG may terminate membership permanently following: a finding of misconduct by the Professional Standards Committee; conviction of a criminal offence relevant to professional standing; persistent non-payment of dues; or submission of materially false information in any application or renewal. Termination is effected by written notice. Members may appeal a termination decision in accordance with CILG's published appeals procedure within 30 days of notification.

11.4 Consequences of Termination

Upon termination of membership, whether voluntary or otherwise, the former Member must: immediately cease to use all CILG credentials and post-nominal designations; remove all references to CILG membership from professional profiles, marketing materials, and communications; and return or destroy any CILG-issued membership certificates upon request. CILG will update the Verification Portal to reflect the change in status.
