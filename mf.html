Section 2

MEMBERSHIP FEE SCHEDULE

2.1 Overview and Policy

The Chartered Institute of Leadership and Governance (CILG) is a professional body whose financial sustainability depends on the contribution of its members through membership subscriptions, upgrade fees, and other professional services charges. All fees collected are used exclusively to fund the delivery of CILG qualifications, professional development services, member benefits, regulatory compliance, and the advancement of the profession.

The CILG fee structure is reviewed annually by the CILG Finance Committee and ratified by the Board of Governors. Fee revisions are communicated to all members at least ninety (90) days before they take effect. The fee schedule set out in this section represents the standard fee schedule for the 2024 financial year.

Currency Notice: All fees are denominated in United States Dollars (USD). Members residing outside the United States are subject to the same fee schedule; however, regional pricing supplements may apply in certain jurisdictions, subject to Board approval and published in the CILG Regional Fee Schedule supplement.
2.2

Membership Upgrade Fees

The Membership Upgrade Fee is a one-time charge payable at the point of application for progression to a new membership grade. This fee covers the administrative cost of application processing, qualification verification, evidence assessment, and conferral of the new grade.

2.3

Annual Membership Subscription Fees

The Annual Membership Subscription is a recurring charge payable each year to maintain active CILG membership in good standing. Subscriptions fund the ongoing delivery of member services, CPD resources, publications, regulatory functions, and the CILG's professional advocacy work.

Subscription Payment Policy
  • Annual subscriptions fall due on the 1st of January each year (or on the anniversary of membership for new members joining mid-year).
  • A grace period of thirty (30) days is provided after the due date before any lapse penalties apply.
  • Members who have not paid by the end of the grace period will receive a formal reminder and a further fourteen (14) days to pay.
  • Membership is placed in 'Lapsed' status after forty-four (44) days of non-payment from the due date.
  • Lapsed members may not use CILG post-nominal designations and lose access to member benefits.
  • Monthly installment payment options are available for Associate, Full Member, and Fellow grades — apply to Membership Services.
  • Student Members do not pay an annual subscription; their membership is maintained through active enrollment in CILG qualification modules.

2.6

Hardship, Waivers, and Concessionary Fees

2.6.1 CILG Hardship Fund

CILG recognizes that financial hardship may, in exceptional circumstances, prevent otherwise qualified and committed professionals from maintaining their membership or pursuing progression. The CILG Hardship Fund provides a mechanism for fee support to members facing demonstrable financial difficulty.

Hardship Fund — Key Terms
Supporting Members Through Genuine Financial Difficulty
  • Eligibility: Current CILG members (at any grade) who can demonstrate genuine financial hardship.
  • Maximum Award: Up to 100% of the annual subscription fee for one year; up to 50% of an upgrade fee.
  • Application: Via a confidential written application to the CILG Secretary-General, with supporting evidence.
  • Decisions are made by the Secretary-General in consultation with the Finance Committee Chair, with full confidentiality.
  • Hardship support may be granted a maximum of two (2) times per member in any five-year period.

2.6.2 – 2.6.4 Concessionary Fee Rates

🌍
2.6.2 Low- and Middle-Income Country (LMIC) Concession
50% Off
Members resident in countries classified as low- or middle-income by the World Bank are entitled to a concessionary fee rate of fifty percent (50%) on both the upgrade fee and annual subscription for their grade. This concession applies automatically on application; members must indicate their country of residence on their membership form. The CILG Finance Committee reviews the list of qualifying countries annually in line with World Bank classifications.
🎓
2.6.3 Early Career Concession
25% Off
Members who have been in full-time professional employment for fewer than three (3) years at the time of application for Affiliate or Associate Membership are entitled to a twenty-five percent (25%) reduction on the applicable upgrade fee. Evidence of employment start date is required.
🎖️
2.6.4 Armed Forces, Government Service, and Non-Profit Concession
20% Off
Members in active military service, full-time public sector government service, or full-time employment with registered non-profit organizations are entitled to a twenty percent (20%) reduction on annual subscriptions at all grades. Verification of employment status is required annually at renewal.
