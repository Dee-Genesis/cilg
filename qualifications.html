Foreword

The Chartered Institute of Leadership and Governance (CILG) is a distinguished professional body incorporated in the United States of America, dedicated to the advancement of professional excellence in leadership, corporate governance, organizational management, and public administration. CILG serves as a standard-setting institution that benchmarks leadership and governance competencies against internationally recognized best practices.

This document sets forth the official CILG Professional Qualification Framework and Membership Framework — two integrated systems designed to define, recognize, and elevate the professional standing of practitioners in the fields of leadership and governance worldwide.

The Qualification Framework is structured across five progressive levels — Foundation, Knowledge, Skills, Professional, and Expert — each comprising five rigorous modules aligned to real-world professional competencies. Unlike purely academic programs, CILG qualifications are explicitly practice-oriented, designed to equip professionals with the analytical, strategic, and ethical capabilities required in modern organizations.

CILG is committed to maintaining the highest standards of professional integrity, competence, and ethics in all aspects of its operations and the conduct of its members.

Professional Qualification Framework

Five Levels. A Lifetime of Professional Authority.

CILG's competency-based qualification framework is built for practitioners, not classrooms. Each level builds demonstrated professional mastery — from foundational governance principles to the Chartered Professional designation at Level 5.

1 Foundation
Certificate in Leadership & Governance
Membership Grade
Student Member (SCILG)
Foundation Level
3–6 Months

The entry point to the CILG framework. Introduces core leadership theory, corporate governance principles, organisational behaviour, and professional ethics. Suitable for early-career professionals and those transitioning into governance roles. No prior experience required.

2 Knowledge
Diploma in Leadership & Governance
Membership Grade
Affiliate Member (AfCILG)
Knowledge Level
6–9 Months

Builds on foundational knowledge with deeper exploration of governance frameworks, financial oversight, board dynamics, stakeholder management, and legal dimensions of governance. Introduces analytical thinking and applied professional judgment.

Explore Level 2 →
3 Skills
Advanced Diploma in Leadership & Governance
Membership Grade
Associate Member (ACILG)
Skills Level
9–12 Months

A professional-grade qualification covering strategic governance, risk oversight, financial stewardship, regulatory environments, and ethical leadership in complex organisations. The first fully qualified CILG grade — ACILG is a recognised professional designation.

Explore Level 3 →
4 Professional
Executive Diploma in Leadership & Governance
Membership Grade
Full Member (MCILG)
Professional Level
12–18 Months

A comprehensive executive-level programme for senior professionals. Covers board effectiveness, ESG governance, institutional strategy, organisational transformation, and advanced corporate governance. Includes a Viva Voce oral assessment and Advanced Professional Portfolio.

Explore Level 4 →
5 Expert
Chartered Professional in Leadership & Governance
Membership Grade
Fellow (FCILG) — The Highest Honour
Expert Level — The Chartered Designation
Senior Executive Programme

The pinnacle of the CILG framework. A master-practitioner level programme for senior executives covering global governance challenges, systemic accountability, transformational leadership, and national/international governance policy. Confers the prestigious FCILG Chartered Professional title.
