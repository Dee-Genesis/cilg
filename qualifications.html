CILG Professional
Qualification Framework

A competency-based, five-level qualification pathway advancing professional excellence in leadership, corporate governance, organizational management, and public administration — aligned to internationally recognised best practices.

📚 25 Core Modules
🌍 International Standard

Section 1

Overview of the CILG
Qualification Framework

The CILG Professional Qualification Framework (PQF) is designed to develop and certify professionals who demonstrate mastery in leadership theory and practice, governance structures and compliance, organizational strategy, ethical decision-making, and executive management. The framework is built on a competency-based model rather than an academic credits model, meaning progression is determined by demonstrated professional knowledge, practical application, and real-world competence.

The CILG PQF draws conceptual alignment from internationally respected professional qualification structures, including those of ACCA, CIMA, CIPD, and the Institute of Directors (IoD), while maintaining a unique identity rooted in leadership and governance practice.

Level Title Qualification Award Membership Outcome Post-nominal Duration
Level 1 Foundation Certificate in Leadership and Governance Student Member None 3–6 months
Level 2 Knowledge Diploma in Leadership and Governance None at this level AfCILG 6–9 months
Level 3 Skills Advanced Diploma in Leadership and Governance Associate Member (ACILG) ACILG 9–12 months
Level 4 Professional Executive Diploma in Leadership and Governance Full Member (MCILG) MCILG 12–18 months
Level 5 Expert Chartered Professional in Leadership and Governance Fellow (FCILG) FCILG ★ 18–24 months

1.3 General Eligibility for the Framework

The CILG qualification framework is open to all individuals who satisfy the minimum entry requirement for their respective level. The framework is professionally oriented; therefore, academic qualifications alone do not guarantee advancement. Professional experience, professional development activities, and demonstrated competence carry significant weight in progression decisions.

📋 Minimum Entry Requirements — Level 1 (Foundation)
  • Age: 18 years and above
  • Academic: High school diploma, GED, or equivalent secondary qualification
  • Professional: No prior professional experience required
  • Language: Proficiency in English (written and spoken)
  • Other: Completion of CILG enrollment form and payment of applicable registration fees

The Foundation Level is the entry point to the CILG Qualification Framework. This level is designed to introduce candidates to the core concepts, principles, and vocabulary of leadership, governance, and organizational management. It is appropriate for early-career professionals, administrators, supervisors, and individuals seeking to transition into leadership roles.

Level 1 Modules (5 Modules)
Module 1.1
Fundamentals of Leadership
Introduces foundational theories and models of leadership, exploring the evolution of leadership thought from classical trait theory to contemporary transformational and servant leadership models.
Evolution of leadership theory: trait, behavioral, contingency, transformational and servant leadership
Leadership versus management — key distinctions and complementary roles
Self-awareness and emotional intelligence as leadership foundations
Communication, motivation, and team dynamics
Ethical leadership and personal integrity
Module 1.2
Introduction to Corporate Governance
Provides a foundational understanding of corporate governance principles, structures, and frameworks. Examines the roles of directors and boards, and the importance of transparency and accountability.
Definition, purpose, and importance of corporate governance
Historical development of governance frameworks
The board of directors: roles, composition, and responsibilities
Shareholder rights and stakeholder theory
Introduction to governance codes and regulations (US governance standards)
Module 1.3
Organizational Behavior and Culture
Examines how individuals and groups behave within organizational settings and how culture shapes organizational performance, decision-making, and change.
Perception, attitudes, personality, and their impact on workplace behavior
Group dynamics, team formation, and team effectiveness
Organizational culture: models, typologies, and culture change
Conflict, negotiation, and resolution in organizational settings
Diversity, inclusion, and the role of leadership in fostering belonging
Module 1.4
Business Communication and Professional Ethics
Develops candidates' ability to communicate professionally and ethically in diverse organizational contexts, while laying the groundwork for ethical professional conduct.
Principles of effective professional written and oral communication
Report writing, presentation, and professional correspondence
Business ethics: frameworks, dilemmas, and decision-making models
Whistleblowing, codes of conduct, and professional integrity
Digital communication and social media responsibilities
Module 1.5
Introduction to Strategy and Organizations
Equips candidates with an understanding of how organizations operate strategically and the basics of organizational management.
What is strategy? Strategic intent, mission, vision, and values
The external environment: PESTLE analysis and competitive forces
Organizational structures and their implications for leadership
Basic resource management: people, finance, and operations
Introduction to organizational performance measurement

The Skills Level represents a significant milestone in the CILG qualification journey. At this level, candidates move beyond theoretical understanding to demonstrate applied professional competence. The Advanced Diploma in Leadership and Governance is a practice-oriented qualification that requires candidates to apply frameworks and concepts to realistic professional scenarios. Successful completion of Level 3 confers the designation of Associate Member of the Chartered Institute of Leadership and Governance (ACILG).

LevelLevel 3 — Skills
Duration9–12 months
Modules5 modules
AssessmentCase study reports, Professional Practice Assignments, Examination
Pass Mark50% per module
MembershipAssociate Member (ACILG)
📋 Entry Requirements — Level 3
  • Completion of CILG Level 2 Certificate (Knowledge Level), OR
  • Recognised equivalent qualification with approved exemption from the CILG Exemptions Committee
  • Minimum two (2) years of relevant professional experience in a leadership, management, governance, or administrative role
  • Current CILG Student membership

Progression to Associate Membership (ACILG)
  1. Pass all 5 modules at Level 3 (minimum 50% per module)
  2. Submit a 2,000-word Professional Competency Reflection linking Level 3 learning to real workplace experience

  4. Pay Associate Membership application fee
  5. Sign CILG Code of Professional Conduct

The Executive Diploma in Leadership and Governance represents senior-level professional mastery. Designed for experienced professionals in leadership and governance roles, this level demands sophisticated analysis, evidence of professional impact, and the ability to lead at an organizational or sectoral level. Successful completion leads to Full Membership of CILG (MCILG).

LevelLevel 4 — Advanced
Duration12–18 months
Modules5 modules

Pass Mark50% per module

📋 Entry Requirements — Level 4
  • Completion of CILG Level 3 Diploma (Skills Level) and current Associate Membership (ACILG), OR

Progression to Full Membership (MCILG)

The Professional Level is the pinnacle of the CILG qualification framework, conferring the prestigious title of Chartered Professional in Leadership and Governance and the designation Fellow of CILG (FCILG). This level is reserved for seasoned professionals who have demonstrated exceptional mastery, strategic leadership, and meaningful contribution to the advancement of leadership and governance practice. No exemptions are permitted at this level.

LevelLevel 5 — Expert
Duration18–24 months
Modules5 modules
AssessmentStrategic Dissertation, Executive Portfolio, Peer Assessment, Oral Defense
Pass Mark50% per module — no exemptions
MembershipFellow (FCILG) — Chartered
📋 Entry Requirements — Level 5
  • Completion of CILG Level 4 Advanced Diploma and current Full Membership (MCILG)
  • Minimum eight (8) years of progressive professional experience in leadership, governance, or executive management
  • At least five (5) years in a senior or executive leadership role (C-suite, Board, Director, Senior VP, or equivalent)
  • Demonstrated contribution to the profession: publications, speaking engagements, board service, policy contributions, or mentoring
  • Endorsement by two (2) existing FCILG Fellows of the Institute
  • Completion of the CILG Professional Entry Interview (conducted by a panel of three FCILG Fellows)
  • Current CILG CPD compliance: minimum 40 CPD hours per year for the preceding two years

Module 5.5 — Capstone
Professional Capstone — Leadership and Governance in Practice
The culminating assessment of the CILG Professional qualification. Candidates produce a substantial, original Professional Governance Report addressing a real strategic, governance, or leadership challenge.
Professional Governance Report: 8,000–10,000 words addressing a substantive leadership or governance challenge
Oral Defense before a CILG Examiners Panel (60 minutes)
Peer Review by one other Level 5 candidate
CPD Portfolio Review confirming professional standing and active practice
Fellowship Conferral Process (FCILG)
  1. Pass all 5 Level 5 modules (including successful Capstone oral defense)
  2. Full membership compliance — no outstanding disciplinary matters
  3. CILG Fellowship Application reviewed by the CILG Admissions and Standards Committee
  4. Final approval by the CILG Board of Governors
  5. Formal Conferral at the CILG Annual Conferment Ceremony (or by postal conferral where attendance is not possible)
  6. Publication of FCILG designation in the CILG Official Register
🏆 Post-nominal: FCILG · Chartered Title: Chartered Professional in Leadership and Governance · The FCILG may also use the title 'Chartered Member' of CILG in all professional communications
Section 1.4

Assessment Philosophy

All CILG assessments are competency-driven and professionally focused. The Institute employs a variety of assessment modalities to ensure holistic evaluation.

✍️
Written Examinations
Testing knowledge recall, professional reasoning, and analytical judgment across all levels of the framework.
📋
Case Study Assessments
Scenario-based evaluations that simulate real organizational and governance challenges, requiring applied problem-solving.
📄
Professional Practice Reports
Documented evidence of applying learned competencies in a professional setting, linking theory to real-world practice.
📁
Portfolio of Evidence
A compilation of work products, professional achievements, and reflective accounts demonstrating sustained competence.
🎤
Oral Assessments / Viva Voce
For Advanced and Professional levels to assess depth of understanding, critical thinking, and professional judgment in dialogue.
📈
Continuing Professional Development (CPD) Records
Required for progression to higher levels, evidencing ongoing professional growth, learning, and engagement.

Section 1.6 – 1.9

Framework Policies

CILG operates clear and structured policies governing exemptions, re-sits, credit transfer, and study modes to ensure fair, transparent, and accessible pathways for all candidates.

🔓
Exemptions Policy
CILG recognises that many candidates already possess significant professional knowledge, prior qualifications, or substantial career experience. The Institute operates a structured Exemptions Policy to avoid unnecessary repetition and to respect the professional credentials of applicants.
Basis for Exemptions
  • Prior Learning Exemptions (PLE): Awarded on the basis of prior qualifications from recognised institutions (universities, leading professional examination bodies, etc.)
  • Professional Experience Exemptions (PEE): Awarded on the basis of substantial verified professional experience in relevant fields
  • Cross-Institutional Recognition: CILG maintains Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) with select international professional bodies; holders of recognised designations from such bodies may qualify for module or level exemptions
🔄
Re-sit Policy
Candidates who fail a module may re-sit up to two (2) times per module. A waiting period of sixty (60) days applies between the original sitting and the first re-sit. Candidates who fail a module three times must formally reapply to that module and pay the full module registration fee.
🤝
Credit Transfer Policy
CILG participates in a Credit Transfer framework with select partner institutions and international professional bodies. Credits earned at CILG may be transferable toward academic or professional programs at partner institutions. Details of specific credit transfer arrangements are published in the CILG Partnerships Register available on the official CILG website.
