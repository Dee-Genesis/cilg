About the Framework

Why Membership
Matters

CILG membership is a mark of professional distinction — recognised globally across leadership, corporate governance, organisational management, and public administration.

The CILG Membership Framework provides a structured, transparent, and professionally credible pathway for individuals to progress through levels of recognised professional standing. Each grade reflects a distinct stage of development and carries specific rights, responsibilities, and expectations.

Membership signals to employers, colleagues, clients, and the public that the holder adheres to the highest standards of practice, is committed to continuous professional development, and operates within the CILG Code of Professional Conduct.

🏛️

Globally Recognised Credentials

Post-nominal designations carry professional weight recognised by employers and institutions worldwide.

📈

Clear Progression Pathway

From Student to Fellow, each grade maps to a specific qualification level with defined entry criteria.

⚖️

Commitment to Integrity

All members adhere to the CILG Code of Professional Conduct, upholding ethics in every practice.

Professional Standards

The CILG Code of
Professional Conduct

All CILG members commit to upholding a rigorous code of professional conduct — the ethical backbone of the Institute's global standing and public trust.

Read Full Code →
01

Integrity

Maintaining honesty and transparency in all professional dealings, avoiding conflicts of interest.

02

Competence

Committing to ongoing professional development and only undertaking work within one's expertise.

03

Accountability

Taking responsibility for decisions and actions, welcoming scrutiny and professional review.

04

Respect & Inclusion

Treating all persons with dignity, promoting diversity, equity, and inclusion in governance practice.

Continuous Professional Development

Grow. Learn.
Advance.

CILG members are expected to engage in Continuous Professional Development (CPD) to maintain and grow their professional standing. The CILG CPD Portal provides structured resources, credits, and tracking tools to make that journey seamless.

CPD requirements vary by grade, ensuring expectations scale appropriately with the level of professional responsibility held by each member.
