Who We Are

A Standard-Setting Institution for the Governance Profession

The Chartered Institute of Leadership and Governance (CILG) is a distinguished professional body incorporated in the United States of America, dedicated to the advancement of professional excellence in leadership, corporate governance, organisational management, and public administration.

CILG serves as a standard-setting institution that benchmarks leadership and governance competencies against internationally recognised best practices. Our Professional Qualification Framework draws conceptual alignment from globally respected professional bodies while maintaining a unique, practice-first identity rooted in real-world governance.

Professional Qualification Framework

Five Levels. A Lifetime of Professional Authority.

CILG's competency-based qualification framework is built for practitioners, not classrooms. Each level builds demonstrated professional mastery — from foundational governance principles to the Chartered Professional designation at Level 5.

1 Foundation
Certificate in Leadership & Governance
Foundation Level
Student Member (SCILG) · 3–6 Months
The entry point to the CILG framework. Introduces core leadership theory, corporate governance principles, organisational behaviour, and professional ethics. Suitable for early-career professionals and those transitioning into governance roles. No prior experience required.
Explore Level 1 →
2 Knowledge
Diploma in Leadership & Governance
Knowledge Level
Affiliate Member (AfCILG) · 6–9 Months
Builds on foundational knowledge with deeper exploration of governance frameworks, financial oversight, board dynamics, stakeholder management, and legal dimensions of governance. Introduces analytical thinking and applied professional judgment.
Explore Level 2 →
3 Skills
Advanced Diploma in Leadership & Governance
Skills Level
Associate Member (ACILG) · 9–12 Months
A professional-grade qualification covering strategic governance, risk oversight, financial stewardship, regulatory environments, and ethical leadership in complex organisations. The first fully qualified CILG grade — ACILG is a recognised professional designation.
Explore Level 3 →
4 Professional
Executive Diploma in Leadership & Governance
Professional Level
Full Member (MCILG) · 12–18 Months
A comprehensive executive-level programme for senior professionals. Covers board effectiveness, ESG governance, institutional strategy, organisational transformation, and advanced corporate governance. Includes a Viva Voce oral assessment and Advanced Professional Portfolio.
Explore Level 4 →

Chartered Professional in Leadership & Governance
Expert Level — The Chartered Designation
Fellow (FCILG) — The Highest Honour
The pinnacle of the CILG framework. A master-practitioner level programme for senior executives covering global governance challenges, systemic accountability, transformational leadership, and national/international governance policy. Confers the prestigious FCILG Chartered Professional title.

Membership Grades

Your Professional Standing, Formally Recognised

CILG's seven membership grades create a transparent, progressive pathway from student entry to the highest honour in the profession. Each grade carries designatory letters, defined rights, and professional obligations that signal exactly where you stand — and where you're headed.

📚
Student Member
For individuals actively enrolled in CILG Level 1 or 2 qualification programmes. Gateway to the CILG professional community.
🔗
Affiliate Member
AffCILG
Awarded on completion of Level 2 or via 3 years' relevant professional experience. Emerging governance professionals.
🏅
Associate Member
ACILG
First fully qualified CILG grade. Completion of Level 3 + verified professional experience. A recognised professional designation.
Most Popular
⭐
Full Member
MCILG
Senior professional grade for experienced practitioners. Requires Level 4, 7+ years' experience, and Viva Voce assessment. Voting rights included.
🏆
Fellow
The pinnacle of CILG recognition. Confers the Chartered Professional title. Awarded for exceptional mastery and lasting contribution to the profession.
🌟
Honorary Fellow
Hon.FCILG
By special award of the CILG Board. Conferred on distinguished individuals of national or international standing in governance or public life.

Code of Professional Conduct

Five Principles. One Standard.

CILG members are held to a binding Code of Professional Conduct built on five foundational principles. These are not aspirational values — they are enforceable obligations that define what it means to carry CILG designatory letters.

⚖️
Integrity
Members act with honesty, transparency, and moral courage in all professional dealings. Integrity is non-negotiable — and publicly verifiable.
🎓
Competence
Members maintain and develop their professional knowledge through continuous learning, CPD compliance, and demonstrated application of expertise.
📋
Accountability
Members accept responsibility for their decisions, acknowledge errors, and uphold the standards that protect the organisations and communities they serve.
🤝
Respect
Members treat colleagues, clients, and stakeholders with dignity — fostering inclusive, equitable governance environments wherever they lead.
🚀
Leadership
Members lead with purpose and vision — championing governance excellence not just in their roles, but in the institutions and communities they influence.
