About CILG

The Chartered Institute of
Leadership and Governance

A registered professional body dedicated to developing the highest standards of leadership competence, governance practice, professional ethics, and evidence-based governance education serving professionals across government, business, and civil society worldwide.

CILG
Est. Professional Body

CILG exists to build the governance professionals that institutions and communities need most. We develop rigorous qualifications, set binding professional standards, and foster a global community committed to integrity, accountability, and purposeful leadership.

Registered in New Mexico, Alaska, and Michigan, CILG serves governance professionals worldwide through its global network of Accredited Learning Partners and recognized professional standards.

Qualifications

A Structured Pathway to
Governance Mastery

Five progressive qualification levels, from foundational principles to advanced strategic governance, each building the knowledge, competencies, and professional credibility you need.

Level 1
Foundation Certificate in Leadership & Governance
An accessible entry point for professionals beginning their governance journey. Covers foundational leadership principles, governance concepts, and organisational accountability.

Level 2
Certificate in Leadership & Governance
Builds on foundational knowledge with deeper exploration of governance frameworks, board dynamics, stakeholder management, and professional ethics.
Read More →
Level 3
Advanced Certificate in Leadership & Governance
A professional-grade qualification covering strategic governance, risk oversight, financial stewardship, and the regulatory environments of modern organisations.
Read More →
Level 4
Diploma in Leadership & Governance
A comprehensive diploma integrating governance theory with practice. Covers board effectiveness, ESG governance, corporate strategy, and institutional leadership.
Read More →
Level 5
Advanced Diploma in Leadership & Governance
The flagship CILG qualification. A master-practitioner level programme for senior professionals covering global governance challenges, systemic accountability, and transformational leadership.
