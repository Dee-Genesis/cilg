Global Mechanisms

How CILG Extends Its Reach

Two primary mechanisms enable CILG to deliver consistent, rigorous professional standards to governance practitioners worldwide.

🏫

Accredited Learning Partners

CILG collaborates with universities, professional associations, training institutes, and corporate learning organisations worldwide to deliver CILG qualifications. Accredited Learning Partners are vetted and approved to ensure they meet CILG's exacting standards for teaching, assessment, and professional conduct. Regular monitoring and evaluation ensure consistent delivery at every partner institution.

🤝

Mutual Recognition Agreements

CILG maintains Mutual Recognition Agreements with peer professional bodies in governance and related fields globally. These agreements facilitate recognition of qualifications and professional standing across jurisdictions — strengthening the professional community and extending the reach of governance excellence to every corner of the world.

International Recognition

Credentials & Designatory Letters

CILG membership is evidenced through designatory letters that reflect professional standing and expertise — recognised by governance institutions, boards, and employers worldwide.

CILG qualifications and professional credentials are recognised by governance institutions, boards, and employers worldwide as evidence of professional competence in governance and leadership. CILG maintains partnerships with peer professional bodies in governance and related disciplines, facilitating mutual recognition of qualifications and supporting the global development of governance professionals.

All CILG members commit to maintaining the professional standards set out in the CILG Code of Ethics and Professional Standards Framework, including ongoing Continuing Professional Development to ensure current knowledge of governance practice and emerging issues.

Quality Assurance

CILG maintains robust quality assurance processes for all qualifications, assessments, and learning delivery. Accredited Learning Partners are subject to regular monitoring, review, and evaluation to ensure consistent delivery of high-quality education in line with CILG standards.

📋
Continuing Professional Development

All members participate in CILG's CPD framework, ensuring ongoing competence in governance practice, emerging regulatory developments, and evolving professional standards across sectors and geographies.
